Our Story

A kitchen built around fire and patience.

Ember & Oak started in 2013 as a single wood oven and a folding table. Today it's still the same idea, just with more chairs.

Everything on the menu changes with the seasons, because that's when it tastes best.

Live-Fire

Every dish touches wood-fired heat.

Seasonal

Rewritten as seasons change.

Local

Sourced within 30 miles.

Meet The Chef

"Fire doesn't lie. It demands respect and simplicity."

Executive Chef Liam Vance spent over a decade working in open-flame kitchens across Europe before returning home to establish Ember & Oak. His philosophy centers around honoring the raw product.

"When you cook with oak and live coals, you can't hide behind heavy sauces. The ingredient speaks for itself."

Chef Liam Vance · Founder & Executive Chef
